One card for all your 3D printers

A Home Assistant dashboard card with a drawn printer and your print growing inside it. Live camera, slicer info, AMS spools in their real colours and controls. Bambu Lab, Klipper, Creality, Anycubic and OctoPrint.

Install with HACS View on GitHub

Everything at a glance

Pick any entity of your printer. The card finds the rest by itself, also after you rename entities.

Drawn printerBambu Lab with AMS, bed slingers, Voron, Creality K1 and Kobra S1 with ACE. The print preview grows inside it in your filament colour.
Live cameraSwitch to the printer's camera on the card. It only streams while you look at it.
Slicer infoMaterial, layer height, layers, filament used, the slicer's estimate and which slicer made it.
AMS & ACE spoolsYour spools in their real colours, the active one outlined.
ControlsPause, resume and cancel (with confirmation), and the printer light.
Printer groupAll printers in one list with progress and finish time. Great for a print farm or a wall tablet.

Light and dark, on every screen

Follows your Home Assistant theme and language. Built to stay light on wall tablets: it only redraws when your printer changes, and the moving toolhead never repaints the drawing.

Install

  1. In HACS: ⋮ → Custom repositories → add https://github.com/Rein033/lovelace-3d-printer-card as Dashboard, then Download.
  2. Edit a dashboard → Add card → search 3D Printer.
  3. Pick your printer in the visual editor. Done.

Prefer YAML?

type: custom:printer-3d-card
entity: sensor.x1c_print_status   # any entity of the printer

type: custom:printer-3d-group-card
title: Printers
